## Step 1 — Core: value an open trade
- **Build:** `app/core/holdings.py` (plain Python, no web imports). `value_trade(trade, short_quote, long_quote, spot, vix..., as_of) -> TradeValue`: `value_now` = (long mid − short mid − `FILL_OFFSET`) × 100 × contracts (what closing now would get; a missing bid or ask is `None`, never 0, and the row says "No quote"), `pnl` and `pnl_pct` against `open_fill × 100 × contracts`, `days` (calendar and trading), break-evens at the short expiry from the trade's strike, expiries and fill price (the same `pricing` functions and `brentq` as the Finder), `to_nearest_be` in points, position delta, theta and vega from the model at mid-implied IVs (`config.MODEL_GREEKS`), `delta_per_contract`, `short_iv`, `long_iv`, `skew_now`. `flags(value) -> list[str]`: **LOSS** at `pnl_pct <= config.FLAG_LOSS`, **TARGET** at `>= config.FLAG_TARGET`, **DELTA** when `abs(delta_per_contract) > config.FLAG_DELTA` (2.0, new). `exit_reason_suggestion(flags) -> "+10% target" | "−10% flag" | "Other"`.
- **Files:** app/core/holdings.py (new), app/core/models.py (`TradeValue`), app/core/config.py, tests/test_holdings.py (new)
- **Check:** hand-made trades and quotes: P/L at the fill is about −$0.10 × 100 × contracts (the close offset) and about $0 once that is added back; a trade at +10.0% gets TARGET and at −10.0% gets LOSS (boundaries included); delta 2.01 per contract gets DELTA, 2.00 does not; a missing bid gives `None` fields and no flags; break-evens equal what `get_risk_profile` gives for the same calendar at the same fill.

## Step 3 — Service: `get_holdings()` and the marks rule
- **Build:** replace the stub in `app/core/services.py`. `get_holdings(store, account="Paper") -> HoldingsResult`: lists open trades, **one batched `get_quotes` call** for every leg plus SPX, VIX and VIX9D (rule 8; the 15–30 s cache is fine here), builds a `TradeValue` per trade, and returns the **summary cards** (SPX now with today's change, open trades, capital in trades, open P/L, Vol P&L today) and the **Closed list** for the chosen window (7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days / All, read from `settings`). **Vol P&L today** = for each leg, vega × (IV now − IV at the previous close) × contracts × 100, using the previous close from `marks` (the short leg's drop helps, the long leg's hurts); `None` with the reason "No previous close yet" in the first days. **Marks rule (CLAUDE.md rule 7):** a marks row is written on every manual Refresh, at most every `config.MARKS_EVERY_S` (300) seconds while Holdings polls, and once at the close snapshot after 4:15 PM ET (`is_close_snapshot = 1`); nothing is written on a failed or partial quote.
- **Files:** app/core/services.py, app/core/models.py (`HoldingsResult`, `HoldingsCards`, `HoldingRow`), tests/test_holdings.py, tests/test_services.py (mocked quotes)
- **Check:** with mocked quotes and two open trades: the cards add up (open P/L is the sum of the rows); a poll within 5 minutes of the last mark writes none, a Refresh always writes one, the 4:15 PM snapshot is written once per day; a trade with a missing quote still appears (as "No quote") and the others are valued; Vol P&L matches a hand calculation and is `None` without a previous close; the Closed window filters by `closed_at`.

## Step 4 — Service: `close_trade()`, closing quote and the API
- **Build:** `quote_close(trade_id)` reuses `quote_spread` for the trade's two leg symbols (symbols are looked up from the trade's expiries and strike) and returns the closing side: bid, ask, mid, `suggested_price` = mid − `FILL_OFFSET` on the $0.05 grid, P/L in $ and % at that price, days held, SPX now. `close_trade(store, trade_id, price, price_source, exit_reason, notes)`: refuses a trade that is not open, a price ≤ 0 and an unknown reason; takes the **exit snapshot** (SPX, VIX, VIX9D, both IVs, skew, Vol P&L as in Step 3) from a fresh quote; saves `exit_reason_suggested` from `exit_reason_suggestion()` **and** the user's `exit_reason` (Performance groups by the user's choice); computes `realised_pnl`, `realised_pnl_pct`, `days_held`; writes a final marks row. `price_source` is "auto" when the price equals the suggestion, "manual" when edited. Routes: `GET /api/holdings`, `GET /api/trades/{id}/close-quote`, `POST /api/trades/{id}/close`, `PUT /api/trades/{id}/notes`, `GET`/`PUT` of the Closed window through the existing settings route. A fill outside the bid–ask is a returned warning, not an error. The stability wait stays off (`config.REQUIRE_STABLE = False`) for closing too and switches on with the same flag.
- **Files:** app/core/services.py, app/api/holdings.py (new), app/api/trades.py, app/api/__init__.py, tests/test_close_trade.py (new), tests/test_routes.py
- **Check:** open then close a trade on mocked quotes: one row, `status = 'closed'`, realised P/L equals (close fill − open fill) × 100 × contracts, the entry fields are untouched; closing twice gives 409; a bad reason gives 422; an expired-login error gives 503 (week 2 handlers); the suggestion for a +10.5% trade is "+10% target" while a manual price keeps the user's own reason.

## Decisions I would make (change any before starting)
1. **Value now = mid − $0.10 (the close offset), P/L against the fill.** Right after opening, the P/L shows about −$0.10 per contract-share; that is honest (closing right away would cost it) and it matches PLAN's "Value now" definition. Flags use this P/L.
2. **Flags use P/L % of cost at close value**, so LOSS and TARGET fire where a close at mid − $0.10 would actually realise them.
3. **DELTA flag at more than 2.0 per contract either way** (`config.FLAG_DELTA`, new), from the model's delta (`MODEL_GREEKS`), not Schwab's rounded one.
4. **Holdings polls every 30 s but writes a marks row at most every 5 minutes**, on every manual Refresh, and at the 4:15 PM close snapshot (CLAUDE.md rule 7); marks are what Vol P&L and week 5's exit-target comparison read.
5. **Vol P&L today is empty in the first days** (it needs a previous close); the cell says so rather than showing 0.
6. **Stability wait stays off for closing**, as for opening (`REQUIRE_STABLE = False`); the price box is editable at any time and a manual price is tagged "manual".
7. **Exit reason: the app suggests, you choose.** Both are saved (`exit_reason_suggested`, `exit_reason`); Performance in weeks 5–6 groups by your choice.
8. **No partial closes, no rolling or adjusting in the app** this week; a close is all contracts. Delta / Adjustment is an exit reason, not a feature.
9. **Paper only; no orders are ever sent.** Copy to TOS and Paste from TOS are the only bridge to thinkorswim.
10. **Slip order if the week runs long:** Step 11, then 10, then 9. Steps 1–8 are the core loop and are not negotiable, because paper trading cannot start without them.
