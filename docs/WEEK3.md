## Step 3 — Services: `quote_spread()` and `open_trade()`
- **Build:** in `app/core/services.py`. **`quote_spread(short_symbol, long_symbol, size_mode, size_value) -> QuoteSpread`**: one `schwab_api.get_quotes([...], use_cache=False)` call (the 15–30 s cache must not serve a stability check), builds the `CalendarQuote`, appends it to an in-memory window kept per leg pair (a small dict guarded by a lock, entries dropped after `WAIT_TIMEOUT_S` of silence), and returns `bid`, `ask`, `mid`, `spread`, `suggested_price` (= mid + `FILL_OFFSET`, rounded to $0.05, the SPX tick for options over $3), `is_stable`, `waiting_reason`, `range_low`, `range_high`, `quotes_seen`, `seconds_waiting`, `give_up` (true after 60 s unstable, for the keep-waiting prompt), and the sizing for that price (`contracts`, `cost`, `margin`). **`open_trade(request) -> Trade`**: validates (both expiries are SPXW dates from `pick_expiries` rules, width 3–7, `contracts >= 1`, price > 0), refuses a duplicate open trade of the same strike and expiries with a clear message, takes the entry snapshot (SPX, VIX, VIX9D from `get_spot()`; IVs, skew and break-evens from the selected `CalendarRow` of the Finder snapshot named in the request; Vol P&L at entry from `finder_log` when a previous close exists, else null) and writes the trade through `Store.insert_trade`. The price source is "auto" when the fill equals the suggestion, "manual" when edited. The bid–ask warning (fill outside bid–ask) is returned as a field, not an error. `Trade` and `QuoteSpread` are Pydantic models in `app/core/models.py`.
- **Files:** app/core/services.py, app/core/models.py, tests/test_services.py (mocked quotes), tests/test_trades.py (new)
- **Check:** with mocked quotes: five steady quotes make `is_stable` true; one jump resets it; `give_up` appears after 60 simulated seconds; the suggested price is mid + $0.10 on the $0.05 grid; `open_trade` saves exactly one row with the fields filled; a manual price is tagged "manual"; a second identical open is refused; a fill outside bid–ask saves with a warning; contracts follow the Size by override, not the Finder's setting.

## Decisions I would make (change any before starting)
1. **Stability window in server memory**, keyed by the two leg symbols, fed by `POST /api/quote-spread` every 2 s (CLAUDE.md rules 7 and 9). A server restart just restarts the wait; nothing is lost because nothing is saved until Confirm.
2. **Suggested price on the $0.05 grid.** Mid + $0.10 is rounded to the nearest $0.05, because SPX options over $3 tick in nickels and thinkorswim would reject other prices. The box still accepts any price you type (tagged manual).
3. **Entry snapshot from the Finder snapshot the dialog was opened from** (IVs, skew, break-evens of the selected row), plus a fresh `get_spot()` for SPX, VIX and VIX9D. If the snapshot has been pruned, the dialog asks you to press Refresh first.
4. **Vol P&L at entry may be empty** in the first days: it needs a previous-close `finder_log` ladder. It is stored as null and labelled, not guessed.
5. **Confirm needs a stable price or your own price.** Typing a price makes the trade "manual" and lets you confirm while the market is still moving.
6. **One open trade per strike and expiry pair per account.** Opening the same calendar again is refused; a different strike or expiry is a different trade.
7. **Paste is open-only this week.** A SELL line is refused with a message; the close dialog and partial closes come in week 5 with Close Trade.
8. **Contracts are never taken from Size By on a pasted trade**, only from the line, as in PLAN.
9. **Risk profile and popovers slip first.** If Steps 1–7 take the whole week, Steps 8 and 9 start week 4 and Holdings follows; I would rather slip them than ship the open flow half-tested.
10. **Fill time in Eastern Time**, like every other timestamp (CLAUDE.md rule 10); a pasted fill can be edited to an earlier time.
